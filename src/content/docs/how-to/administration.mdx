# Administration

**Administration** is available to every signed-in user. It holds the organisations, units and
subscriptions behind your projects and datasets, together with their charges and usage reports.

What you can change there depends on your role. Ordinary actions you cannot use are disabled with a
reason rather than removed, so it is clear what relationship or role a task requires; actions that
only a platform administrator can ever take are hidden instead. Where a read is refused, the page
keeps everything it can still show and says which part is missing — being unable to read an
organisation's members costs you its members, not the page.

## Finding your way around

Administration is scoped to the organisation you are working as, which is chosen in the masthead
rather than here.

A rail down the left holds, for that organisation:

- **Overview** - what the organisation is, what it holds, and the actions that create things in it.
- **Charges** - the organisation's charge ledger. This entry is not offered for the **Default**
  organisation, because charges are refused for it outright.
- **Usage & Inventory** - the read-only usage report across the organisation's units.

Beneath those is the organisation's list of **Units**, with a search field above it. Your own
**personal unit** is marked as such, and each other unit shows its member count and whether it is
private. Choosing a unit opens that unit.

The rail never leaves the screen, so moving between a unit's access, its spend and its usage costs
three clicks rather than three searches. On narrow viewports the rail collapses and the unit list
becomes part of the **Overview** page.

## Overview

The organisation's identity and its identifier, then the actions that create things inside it:

- **Create unit** - a new unit in this organisation, if you are allowed one there. The **Default**
  organisation cannot have units added to it other than each user's own personal unit.
- **Create personal unit** - your own unit in the **Default** organisation. This is how you get one;
  you are not given one automatically.
- **Create organisation** - a new organisation.

Below those, for an organisation you are a member of, are its **Default project privacy** and its
**Members**. If you are not a member, the page says so and shows the rest.

## A unit

A unit has four sections, presented as tabs beneath its name:

- **Access** - the unit's **Name**, its **Default project privacy**, its **Members**, and its
  **Deletion**.
- **Subscriptions** - the subscriptions held in this unit, searchable, with the option to create
  one. A subscription can be renamed, have its **Allowance** increased — an allowance can be
  increased but never reduced — and be deleted.
- **Charges** - the unit's charge ledger.
- **Usage & Inventory** - the read-only usage report for this unit.

## Subscriptions

A subscription is what a project or a dataset store is billed under; it is the Account Server's
_product_, and the Product ID is still shown in the technical details.

A subscription states its **Product type**, **Tier**, when it was **Created**, its **Billing day**,
its **Allowance** and its **Limit**. It has two sections of its own: **Subscription** and
**Charges**.

A project's own subscription is reachable directly from that project — **View subscription** and
**View charges** in its **[Manage](/docs/how-to/projects/manage)** section.

## Charges

**Charges** holds the detailed, read-only ledgers. Charges accumulate over a billing cycle of a
month, and you choose which cycle you are looking at.

Each ledger is broken into two parts: **PROCESSING** charges (the work that was run) and
**STORAGE** charges (assessed on a daily basis).

Note in particular the _Charge_ column in the processing table. This shows the cost of the work you
ran. You will see that not everything has an associated cost.

## Usage & Inventory

**Usage & Inventory** is a read-only report. For an organisation it accounts for the users across
its units. For a unit it can be read two ways — **By user** or **By project** — and states, for each
user, their unit membership, the datasets they own or edit, and the projects they observe, edit or
administer, along with when they were first and last seen and whether they have used the API.

A user appears in a report once they belong to a unit or hold a role in one of its projects.

For one project's own usage and spend, use that project's
**[Manage](/docs/how-to/projects/manage)** section instead.
